# Age Badge (Under 18 / Under 21)

The Cashier can show a small colored badge next to a member's name: **UNDER 18** or **UNDER 21**. It helps your staff see a customer's age group at the counter.

The badge is an identification aid only. ManageRegister does not enforce, block or make any compliance decision based on it. Your staff make all eligibility, rental and compliance decisions.

---

## What the Badges Mean

| Badge | Color | The member is |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| **UNDER 18** | Red | Under 18 |
| **UNDER 21** | Amber (orange) | 18, 19 or 20 |
| No badge | None | 21 or older, or has no date of birth on file |

A member gets one badge at most. A member under 18 shows **UNDER 18** only.

The badge is worked out from the member's date of birth.

---

## Where You See It

- **Member 1.** On the Cashier, the **Member 1** card shows the member's name, then the badge.
- **Extra shooters.** Click **+ Add second shooter** to add a second person to the sale. They get their own **Member 2** card with their own badge. The link then changes to **+ Add third shooter**. A sale can have up to 3 shooters.
- **Member search.** Click the **Member 1** area to open the **Search member** pop-up. Search by name, email or code. In the results, a member with a badge has it as plain text after their name, for example:

  `Alex Doe (Free) — alex@example.com [UNDER 18]`

When you add a second shooter, the order **Note** fills in by itself, for example *2 people: Alex Doe, Sam Lee*.

---

## It Never Blocks a Sale

The badge is for information. No pop-up appears and the sale is not blocked. Select the member, add the items and take payment as usual.

For the members the Cashier does block, see [Paused and Inactive Members](./paused-and-inactive-members.md).

---

## No Badge Does Not Mean the Customer Is an Adult

The badge needs a date of birth on file. A member with no date of birth gets no badge, even if they are under 18. Follow your gym's policy for checking age.

---

## Where the Date of Birth Comes From

The date of birth comes from ManageMemberships. ManageRegister reads it when you search for a member in the Cashier.

To enter or change it in ManageMemberships:

1. Go to **Membership → Members** and open the member.
2. On the **Overview** tab, open **Contact Information**.
3. Fill in **Birthday**.
4. Click **Save**.

Then search for the member again in the Cashier.

ManageMemberships also has a **Require Birthdate** switch under **Settings → Portal Settings → Member Requirements**. When it is on, members must enter their birthdate when they sign up. See **Require Birthdate** in the ManageMemberships help.

---

## Members Without a Badge

A member created only in ManageRegister, with **Create Member** or **Quick Add Member**, has no date of birth, so the Cashier shows no badge for them. See [Members and Guests](./members-and-guests.md).

---

## Receipts

The badge is not shown on receipts.

---

## Tips

- If a badge looks wrong, check the member's **Birthday** in ManageMemberships.
- A missing badge only means there is nothing to show. It does not confirm the customer's age.
